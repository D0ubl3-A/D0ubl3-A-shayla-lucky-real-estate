Privacy policy

How this website handles lead information.

Information collected

When a visitor submits a private strategy request, the website may collect the visitor's name, email address, phone number, timeline, area, budget range, property address, message, referral source and campaign parameters.

How it is used

Submitted information is used to respond to the visitor's real-estate request, organize follow-up and understand which marketing sources generate inquiries.

Storage

Lead information is stored in the website's local SQLite database. The administrator is responsible for securing hosting, backups and credentials.

Sharing

This website does not include a system for selling submitted lead data.
