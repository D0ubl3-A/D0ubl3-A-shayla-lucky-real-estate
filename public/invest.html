Investor strategy

Buy the plan—not just the price tag.

Detroit can create compelling investment opportunities, but low acquisition cost alone does not make a property a strong investment.

Build the decision before the transaction.

✓Start with the exit: hold, rent, house-hack, improve, refinance or resell.
✓Use realistic assumptions: rent, vacancy, repairs, utilities, insurance and taxes can change the deal.
✓Inspect the expensive systems: roof, structure, electrical, plumbing and mechanicals matter more than cosmetic finishes.
✓Match the property to the strategy: multifamily, single-family and value-add opportunities require different underwriting.
